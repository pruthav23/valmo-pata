    Orders leaving the hub with a verified pin (7-day average)

    How effort is modelled (assumptions, to be measured in a pilot): without a pin, riders search by the address text: about 4 min for a complete address, 8 for an incomplete one, 12 for landmark-only, with 0–2 calls to the customer. A verified pin takes the rider straight to the door, about 1.5 min. Address-related failures average 3.1% of orders (the case pack's 17% × 18% share), are about 5 times likelier on a home's first order than on repeat orders, and fall by 90% with a verified pin. A delivery takes 9 min plus search time, which sets deliveries per rider-hour. A saved parcel avoids ₹120 of reverse cost.

    Team Prod Gods, IIT Kanpur · Meesho DICE S3 · Valmo Bharosa. Homes, customers and riders are simulated. The matching rules, pin verification and DIGIPIN encoding are the real prototype logic.
